# Recommended Daily Intake (RDI) Reference Tables
## Approximate values for healthy adults — Source: ICMR/WHO guidelines

### Calories (Estimated Energy Requirements)

| Group | Calories/day |
|---|---|
| Adult male, sedentary (18-29 yr) | 2100 kcal |
| Adult male, moderately active | 2400 kcal |
| Adult male, active | 2700 kcal |
| Adult female, sedentary (18-29 yr) | 1700 kcal |
| Adult female, moderately active | 1900 kcal |
| Adult female, active | 2100 kcal |
| Older adult (60+) male | 2000 kcal |
| Older adult (60+) female | 1600 kcal |

### Macronutrients (% of total energy)
- Carbohydrates: 50-60% of calories
- Protein: 10-15% of calories (minimum 0.8g/kg body weight; 1.6-2.2g/kg for muscle gain)
- Fat: 20-30% of calories (limit saturated fat to <10%)
- Fiber: 25-38g/day (women 25g, men 38g)

| Nutrient | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|

| Iron | 17 mg | 21 mg (higher due to menstruation) |

| Vitamin D | 600 IU | 600 IU |
| Vitamin B12 | 2.4 mcg | 2.4 mcg |
| Folate | 400 mcg | 400 mcg (600 mcg pregnant) |
| Potassium | 3500 mg | 3500 mg |
| Magnesium | 400 mg | 310 mg |

### Protein Targets by Goal
- Weight loss: 1.2-1.5g per kg body weight
- Maintenance: 0.8-1.0g per kg body weight
- Muscle gain: 1.6-2.2g per kg body weight
- Elderly (60+): 1.0-1.2g per kg body weight (to prevent sarcopenia)

### Water Intake
- Adults: ~2-3 litres/day (including food water)
- More in hot climates and during exercise

## Allergy and Intolerance Reference

### Lactose Intolerance
- Avoid: cow milk, regular yogurt, paneer, cream, ice cream
- Safe alternatives: lactose-free milk, plant milks (soy/almond/oat), hard cheeses (lower lactose), lactase enzyme supplements
- Calcium alternatives: fortified plant milks, sesame seeds, leafy greens, broccoli, almonds, tofu (calcium-set)

### Gluten Intolerance / Celiac Disease
- Avoid: wheat (atta, maida), barley, rye, semolina (suji), regular oats
- Safe grains: rice, millet (bajra, jowar, ragi), corn, quinoa, amaranth, certified gluten-free oats
- Indian gluten-free staples: rice, ragi roti, jowar roti, bajra roti, idli, dosa (rice-based)

### Nut Allergy (Peanuts/Tree Nuts)
- Avoid: peanuts, almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios, all nut butters
- Protein alternatives: seeds (sunflower, pumpkin, hemp), legumes, eggs, lean meats, soy products

### Egg Allergy
- Avoid: eggs, mayonnaise, egg-containing baked goods
- Protein alternatives: legumes, dairy, soy, lean meats, seeds

## Food Groups and Portions (Indian Context)

### Typical Indian Meal Composition
- Roti: 2-3 rotis = 240-360 kcal, ~6-9g protein
- Dal: 1 katori (150g cooked) = ~175 kcal, ~10g protein
- Rice: 1 katori (150g cooked) = ~195 kcal, ~4g protein
- Sabzi (vegetable curry): 1 katori = 80-150 kcal
- Curd: 1 katori (100g) = ~61 kcal, 3.5g protein
